THIS IS ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS UNBROKEN TAKE. There are NO cuts, NO edits, NO shot changes and NO transitions at any point. One camera, one move, from the first frame to the last.

A close static shot of a window pane at night, seen from inside. Rain runs down the outside of the glass in irregular rivulets, and individual droplets catch the light and slide down, merging into each other as they fall. Beyond the glass and far out of focus, a street of neon signage glows in red, magenta and cold blue, broken into soft round bokeh circles by the wet glass. Occasional headlights track slowly across the background. The room side of the glass is dark. The camera holds completely still on the pane for the entire take while the water keeps moving.

Audio: steady rain against glass, a low distant hum of traffic, no music.
