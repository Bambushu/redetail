THIS IS ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS UNBROKEN TAKE. There are NO cuts, NO edits, NO shot changes and NO transitions at any point. One camera, one move, from the first frame to the last.

A motocross rider attacks a dry dirt track in hard afternoon light, shot from the side as the camera pans fast to keep up with him. The rear knobbly tyre spins hard and throws a continuous rooster tail of loose dirt and gravel back into the air behind the bike, individual clods tumbling and scattering as they fly. The rider is in full gear, mud-flecked jersey and boots, elbows high, body shifting over the bike as the suspension compresses and rebounds over the rutted ground. He hits a rise, the front wheel lifts, the bike goes light and lands hard, and a burst of dust blows out sideways from under both tyres. Sunlight catches the flying dust and the spinning spokes. Dry scrub and a chain-link fence streak past out of focus in the background as the camera whips along with him. The camera never stops moving and never cuts.

Audio: a two-stroke engine screaming and changing pitch, tyres tearing at loose dirt, wind buffeting the microphone.
