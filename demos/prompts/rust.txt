THIS IS ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS UNBROKEN TAKE. There are NO cuts, NO edits, NO shot changes and NO transitions at any point. One camera, one move, from the first frame to the last.

A slow lateral drift across the surface of an old workshop machine in daylight. The metal is heavily weathered: orange rust blooms through chipped green paint, the edges of the paint curl and flake, and fine scratches run across the bare steel underneath. A row of hexagonal bolt heads passes through frame, each ringed with dark grease. Dust sits in the pitted surface. Light comes from a window off to the right, raking across the metal at a low angle so every bump and flake casts its own small shadow. The camera tracks smoothly sideways the entire time without stopping.

Audio: quiet workshop room tone, a distant compressor running, no music.
