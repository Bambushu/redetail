THIS IS ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS UNBROKEN TAKE. There are NO cuts, NO edits, NO shot changes and NO transitions at any point. One camera, one move, from the first frame to the last.

A slow steady push forward low across a forest floor in early morning. Thick green moss covers fallen logs, its surface made of thousands of tiny separate fronds. Fern leaves cross the frame in the near foreground, each leaflet edged with a thin line of dew. Behind them the rough vertical bark of old trees carries deep cracks and pale lichen patches. Shafts of low sun cut diagonally through the canopy from the left, and fine dust and insects drift slowly through the light. The ground is scattered with brown leaf litter and small pale mushrooms. The camera glides forward at a walking pace the whole time, never stopping, never cutting.

Audio: quiet woodland ambience, layered birdsong, a light breeze moving leaves, no music.
